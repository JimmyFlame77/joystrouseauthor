## A Gripping and Emotionally Charged Novel

**_Beyond the Broken Window_**, by **Joy Strouse**, is a gripping and emotionally charged novel set in Baltimore County during the 1970s and 80s. This suspenseful story weaves mystery, psychological tension, love, sensuality, grief, and personal growth into a powerful narrative.

At the heart of _Beyond the Broken Window_ is Julie Fairburn, an 18-year-old who meets Rick Madsen and embarks on a journey filled with hope and turmoil.

## Exploring Trauma and Generational Abuse

The novel explores the dark realities behind Rick’s traumatic past. As Julie and Rick navigate their relationship amid a backdrop of family secrets, drugs, and rock ‘n’ roll culture,

_Beyond the Broken Window_ reveals a shocking family tragedy that exposes long-buried secrets of violence and murder.

## The Struggle to Find Freedom

_Beyond the Broken Window_ delves deeply into the complexities of escaping an abusive marriage, highlighting the lingering impact of trauma and the struggle for freedom. Rick’s emotional instability challenges Julie’s resilience, demonstrating how victims often remain bound by their past.

## A Powerful Story of Family, Friendship, and Healing

This powerful story captures the raw truths of family, loyalty, and friendship, all set against a richly detailed period backdrop. For readers seeking a suspenseful, thought-provoking novel, _Beyond the Broken Window_ offers an unforgettable exploration of human relationships and the enduring effects of trauma.

### From GoodReads
